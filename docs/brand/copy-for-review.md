## Good Faith Estimate (`/good-faith-estimate`)

*Legal page — required federal disclosure under the No Surprises Act. Wording largely follows the federal template.*

### GFE § Header
- **Eyebrow:** Good Faith Estimate
- **Headline (H1):** Your right to a Good Faith Estimate.

### GFE § 1 Federal notice
- **Body (lede):** Under Section 2799B-6 of the Public Health Service Act (the federal *No Surprises Act*), health care providers must give clients who don't have insurance, or who aren't using insurance, an estimate of the bill for medical items and services.

### GFE § 2 What you have the right to
- **Heading:** What you have the right to
- **Item 1:** You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate explaining how much your care will cost.
- **Item 2:** You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate in writing at least one business day before your service. You can also ask your health care provider, and any other provider you choose, for a Good Faith Estimate before you schedule.
- **Item 3:** If you receive a bill that is at least $400 more than your Good Faith Estimate, you can dispute the bill.
- **Item 4:** Make sure to save a copy or picture of your Good Faith Estimate.

### GFE § 3 How to request your estimate
- **Heading:** How to request your estimate
- **Intro line:** To request a Good Faith Estimate, contact Release Wellness directly:
