# Brand identity

## Name

**Release Wellness** (two words, both capitalized in the wordmark).

The legal/footer form in the mockup reads "Release Wellness, LLC."

## Tagline

> Using elements of nature to nurture.

Set in tracked uppercase below the wordmark in the logo lockup.

## Logo

A circular emblem with a stylized tree (full canopy, visible trunk) rendered in sage green with a wood-grain / cross-section texture inside the circle. The circle reads as a tree-ring slice with the tree growing from it — nature, growth, grounding.

**Lockup:** logo mark above wordmark "Release Wellness" (display serif), with a thin horizontal divider broken by a small leaf glyph, and the tagline beneath in tracked uppercase sans.

**Variants seen in the mockups:**
- Full vertical lockup (logo + wordmark + tagline) — homepage hero, business card, signage
- Horizontal lockup (small mark + wordmark stacked with tagline) — site header, footer, social avatar
- Mark only (circular tree emblem) — favicon / social profile picture candidate

## Voice & tone

The voice is **warm, grounded, plainspoken, and reassuring** — never clinical, never cute. It speaks directly to someone who is hesitant to reach out, and feels human rather than corporate or wellness-influencer.

**Avoid:** jargon, hard-sell CTAs, exclamation points, "transform your life" hype, anything that feels like a wellness influencer. Therapy credentials and modalities should be present where relevant, but framed in human terms.

**Pronouns:** first-person singular ("I believe…", "My work…"). Confirmed in her About-page draft (see [copy-bank.md](copy-bank.md)).

## Credentials & required disclosures

The practitioner holds:

- **MSW** — Master of Social Work (degree)
- **ACSW** — Associate Clinical Social Worker, registered with the California Board of Behavioral Sciences (BBS). This is the **pre-licensed** stage on the path to LCSW. Practice is conducted under the supervision of a licensed clinician.

She practices under the supervision of a **Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist (LMFT)** — cross-discipline supervision is permitted by the CA BBS.

### What this means for the website

California BBS regulations (16 CCR §1811) require associate clinicians to disclose their registration status and supervisor in advertising — this includes the website. Plan to include the following on the site (most commonly on the About page **and** in the footer):

- Her name and credential title (e.g., "Jane Doe, MSW, ACSW")
- Her **ASW registration number**
- The fact that she is an associate working under supervision
- Her **supervisor's name, license type (LMFT), and license number**

**Open data we still need before publishing:**
- [ ] Her full legal name as she wants it displayed on the site
- [ ] Her ASW registration number
- [ ] Supervisor's full name + LMFT license number

A common, plain-language phrasing that satisfies the rule:

> Jane Doe, MSW, ASW #00000
> Associate Clinical Social Worker
> Supervised by [Supervisor Name], LMFT #00000

We can soften the visual presentation, but the substance has to be present and legible.
